## Use For

Theme-aware styling, typography, iconography, visual hierarchy, semantic resources, and avoiding unnecessary custom surfaces.

## Prefer

- Theme resources and semantic brushes.
- Light and dark support by default.
- High-contrast-aware states.
- Framework-default control chrome before custom templates.
- Typography and spacing before extra borders.
- Consistent icon style.

## Avoid

- Hard-coded light/dark colors.
- Rebuilding a design system unnecessarily.
- Styling every control from scratch.
- Redundant cards, borders, and containers.
- Visual decoration that hides focus, selection, disabled, or error states.

## Review

- Are resources theme-aware?
- Does the app work in light, dark, and high contrast?
- Are custom surfaces justified?
- Are interaction states visible?
