# USDA and Forest Service priorities briefing
**As of October 20, 2026.** This briefing uses only the supplied packet, which is dated to that day; I did not verify the sites live.

**USDA department strategy.** The supplied [Our Performance page](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/general-information/our-performance) says the full FY 2026-2030 plan is in development and links the available [USDA FY 2026-2030 Strategic Plan Framework](https://usda.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/documents/usda-fy-2026-2030-strategic-plan-framework.pdf). Page 1 summarizes six department goals, including strengthening rural economies and natural resources, and improving customer service and taxpayer value through efficient operations and technology. The FY 2022-2026 plan is listed under past plans, so it is historical in this evidence. The framework is not the completed full plan.

**Forest Service agency priorities.** The [Forest Service priorities page](https://www.fs.usda.gov/about-agency/priorities) covers safety, wildfire, forest management, energy and minerals, disaster recovery, recreation access, organizational efficiency, and shared stewardship. Its recreation section covers campgrounds, facilities, roads, trails, and visitor service; its efficiency section calls for reducing administrative burdens and layers.

**FY26 leadership direction.** Chief Tom Schultz’s [FY26 priorities article](https://www.fs.usda.gov/inside-fs/leadership/focus-fundamentals-fy26-agency-priorities), published January 16, 2026, reinforces those agency-wide topics and gives leadership emphasis for FY26. Its fiscal-year scope does not establish FY27 direction. The packet provides no FY27 leadership document, and its dated excerpts cannot establish whether newer direction was published or whether these pages have since changed.
