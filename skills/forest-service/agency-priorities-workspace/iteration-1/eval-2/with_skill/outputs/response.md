## Revised FY26 justification

The campground maintenance dashboard tracks broken facilities and repair status. That function supports the Forest Service priority to improve recreation facilities and campground service by helping staff see repair needs and their status. It may help staff coordinate service, but the supplied evidence does not show an outcome. The dashboard has no AI feature. The packet establishes no USDA requirement to use AI in every dashboard, no funding guarantee, and no official 50% processing-time target. This project does not demonstrate alignment with every USDA goal.
