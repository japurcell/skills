# Accomplishment report: campground repair dashboard

## Outcome

All plan tasks and acceptance checks are complete. The repair queues and status export shipped, and all 18 acceptance tests passed. Deployment and adoption were outside this work's scope, so this report makes no claim about downstream use or operational results.

## Estimated effort and cost comparison

The supplied human-only work-package estimates total 24 hours: 4 hours for design, 12 for implementation, 6 for verification, and 2 for documentation. At the supplied fully burdened rate of $100 per hour, the estimated human-only cost is $2,400.

Assisted human effort was 6 hours, including prompting, review, testing, and corrections. At the same rate, that is $600. Scoped AI billing, including agents and retries, was $40. The base-case assisted cost is therefore $640, for estimated net savings of $1,760 compared with the $2,400 human-only estimate. That is an estimated cost reduction of 73.3%, alongside 18 fewer human hours.

This is an estimate from the supplied inputs, not a measured financial outcome. The calculation assumes the human-only estimates cover the same completed scope, both effort totals use the same $100 hourly rate, the $40 includes all AI charges, and there are no other costs. The 10 agent runtime hours are not added as human labor or another charge because their billing is included in the $40. Their runtime overlapped within 4 hours of elapsed delivery time; elapsed time is not used as a labor-hour total.

## Sensitivity

Holding assisted effort, hourly rate, and AI billing fixed, a one-way 20% change in the human-only estimate gives:

| Human-only estimate | Human-only cost | Assisted cost | Estimated net savings |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 19.2 hours (-20%) | $1,920 | $640 | $1,280 (66.7%) |
| 24 hours (base case) | $2,400 | $640 | $1,760 (73.3%) |
| 28.8 hours (+20%) | $2,880 | $640 | $2,240 (77.8%) |

At the base-case estimate, AI billing could rise to $1,800 before the assisted cost reaches the $2,400 human-only estimate, assuming assisted human effort stays at 6 hours and no other costs arise. This is $1,760 above the supplied $40 charge. The result is most sensitive to whether the human-only and assisted effort estimates cover comparable work and whether omitted costs or follow-on work are material.

## Scope and follow-up

The report supports completion of the dashboard work and an estimated effort-cost comparison. It does not establish deployment, adoption, or savings from improved campground operations. Those outcomes require evidence from work after this plan's stated scope.
