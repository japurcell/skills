# Internal Web/App Surface

Use for intranet, behind-firewall, or internal operational tools.

## Must follow

- FSDP remains the default look and feel.
- Internal products have more flexibility than public ones, but still need Forest Service identity.
- Keep USFS shield prominent, usually upper-left and first in the app identity.
- Footer structure may be adapted for internal users.
- Do not use “internal” as a reason for weak accessibility, missing labels, inconsistent navigation, or poor readability.

## HTML accessibility

- Use one `h1`.
- Keep heading order logical.
- Ensure keyboard access and visible focus.
- Tie labels to controls.
- Keep enhanced controls usable when JavaScript fails.
