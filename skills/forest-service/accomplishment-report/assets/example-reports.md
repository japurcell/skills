# Example Accomplishment Reports

- Released on October 5th, v1.27.0 of GrowHub was deployed to production, delivering the new plant shipping capability and supporting completion of key seedling workflow functionality. The release advances continued modernization of Forest Service nursery operations by improving the tools used to manage seedling production, fulfillment, and shipping activities. This accomplishment supports the Chief's priorities of active forest management, reforestation, and operational efficiency by enhancing systems that support the production and distribution of seedlings used in restoration efforts.
- Released on October 6th, v2.5 of the Research Information Tracking System (RITS) improved publication management workflows through enhancements to research theme classification, publication processing, fiscal year handling, and CrossRef submission support. These improvements strengthen the reliability and maintainability of systems used to manage Forest Service research information and support the agency's commitment to modern, dependable digital services for scientific information management.
- Released on October 7th, the updated Emergency Medical Services  (IEMS Automation v1.0.57 & IEMS Enhanced Site v1.0.52) delivered enhancements to registration and enrollment workflows, improved certificate generation functionality, and administrative workflow improvements that streamline the management of incident personnel records and qualifications. These enhancements improve operational efficiency and usability for personnel and administrators while supporting the agency's priorities of workforce readiness, operational effectiveness, and modernization of mission-support systems.
- Forest Service personnel began training and adoption activities for newly deployed agreement modification capabilities within the Integration of Incident Procurement Automation (IIPA), including recently released contract modification functionality. These activities support continued operational use of the modernized procurement platform and help improve the efficiency and consistency of incident procurement processes. This effort supports agency priorities focused on workforce readiness, operational efficiency, and modernization of mission-critical business systems.
