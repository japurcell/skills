# Dream

Dream reviews one assigned foreground batch through `start`, `prepare`, optional `publish`, and `complete`. Use an integration-issued `--invocation-file`; absent or stale authority fails before semantic input. `start` returns the exact primary identities/revisions, complete guidance and required references, measured UTF-8 size, and assigned scope. The CLI launches no model.

Activation makes the first cycle due. Eligible startup, task, resume, and recovery events detect the configurable cadence, defaulting to seven UTC calendar days and coalescing missed intervals. One routine batch is assigned at a ready work-session checkpoint. Clarifications do not start a full semantic pass. After learn completes, the next eligible event supplies the dream registration. Unfinished assigned dream or child obligations prevent session completion; unassigned finite cycle coverage can remain due while a session completes.

Review every primary revision and its required references. The default limit is five primaries and 32 KiB of complete UTF-8 guidance, including delivered applicability/evidence/reference metadata. `content_bytes` is `markdown_content_bytes` plus `guidance_metadata_bytes`; it excludes the procedure, result envelope and repeated deliveries, which workload measurements count separately. A rotating quiet primary receives a reserved slot when available. New signals cannot expand the frozen target set or starve quiet targets. Later identities are explicit next-cycle work. Changed inputs invalidate credit and require a current revision review.

An indivisible oversized closure runs alone, reports `oversized`, and retains its complete content and measurement. Unavailable content remains an assigned incomplete obligation. Never truncate it, omit a required reference, or infer an empty queue from a gap.

Prepare a scoped review like [the dream example](../examples/dream-review-v1.json). Its `dispositions` must name every exact assigned primary once, using the batch's target revision. State `reviewed`, `unresolved`, or `resolved`, separately from factual verification `verified`, `uncertain`, or `unavailable`. Reviewing a candidate does not verify its claim. Preserve uncertainty and inaccessible evidence; unavailable access never supports pruning. A no-change review credits only that checked batch after successful output delivery.

For a change, use the evidenced [learn proposal procedure](learn.md), adding the same dispositions. Changes stay within the delivered batch closure. Resolve a candidate only through a checked publication that actually changes that target. Its disposition and supporting evidence enter portable history before removal from the active queue. Pruning requires error, obsolescence or complete redundancy evidence. Preserve policy meaning, useful qualifications/counterexamples, stable references, and exact reversible bytes.

Complete reruns actual checks and verifies the prepared review or published result. A cycle's periodic clock resets only when all finite target credits remain valid at current revisions and its assigned obligations and structural checks settle. Successful recovery output can settle the exact prior task before a new task's context. Failed output earns no credit and retains visible recovery work.

At eligible events, operational cleanup retires only closed, unreferenced records at least 30 UTC calendar days after closure. It retains pending obligations, unresolved candidates, live ownership, publication/recovery, portable evidence/inverse history, and explicit validation pins. It never removes the active database or identity sentinel. `status` reports due coverage, assigned outcomes, and pending cleanup files. No idle job, daemon, or maintenance subagent executes this work.
