1. Identify the hook’s owner and implementation. Determine whether it comes from repository configuration, user configuration, an extension, or the tool host. Its location is not known.

3. Determine the scanned input. Establish whether the failure concerns arguments, resource contents, session context, or scanner initialization.
4. Check ordinary failure causes. Missing scanner executable, permissions, unavailable dependencies, input-size limits, timeouts, malformed requests, and result-parsing failures are possibilities, not diagnoses.
